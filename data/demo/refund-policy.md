# Refund Policy — CiteRAG (Sample)

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Annual Subscription Refunds

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of the initial purchase date. After 30 days, no refunds are available for the remaining months of the subscription term.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. If the card used for purchase has been cancelled or replaced, the refund will be issued as account credit.

## Monthly Plans

Monthly plans are non-refundable. You may cancel at any time; your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

## Enterprise Agreements

Custom enterprise agreements have their own refund terms negotiated at contract signing. Please refer to your master services agreement.

## How to Request a Refund

Contact **billing@citerag.com** or use the Billing tab in your account dashboard. Include your invoice ID. Requests are processed within 2 business days.
