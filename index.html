How It Works

Fix the retrieval layer.
Not just the prompt.

Most "RAG fixes" tweak prompts and hope. We rebuild the four layers that actually determine answer quality.

⚙ Hybrid Retrieval

Vector search misses exact identifiers: product codes, error numbers, policy references. BM25 plus QueryFusionRetriever (reciprocal_rerank) merges both lists.

⇅ Cross-Encoder Reranking

If COHERE_API_KEY is set, CohereRerank keeps the top-N after fusion. If not, hybrid search still runs. Rerank is optional, not required to demo.

Frequently asked
questions.

How is this different from LangChain or LlamaIndex?

Those are kits. This is a finished citation path on LlamaIndex: hybrid retrieve, forced cites, claim verification, refusal, and an eval harness.

⌄

What does an accuracy audit include?

A golden dataset built from your real documents, baseline scores across faithfulness/precision/citation accuracy, and a prioritized fix list. Fixed price, delivered in days.

⌄

Can you fix our existing RAG, or do we have to rebuild?

Most engagements are fixes, not rebuilds. We work inside your current stack (LangChain, LlamaIndex, custom) and replace only what's dragging the score down.

⌄

Which LLMs do you support?

GPT-4o, Claude, Command R+, and open-source models via Ollama — chosen per workload based on measured faithfulness, not hype.

⌄

Is the hosted API always hot?

No. The Render box can cold-start. The demo may wait. Local uvicorn is the reliable path.

⌄

What happens after the fix?

Optional monthly retainer: continuous evaluation on live traffic, regression alerts, and a monthly accuracy report your stakeholders can actually read.
