Questions I get first.

Is this LangChain or LlamaIndex?

Those are kits. This is a finished, evaluated system built on that class of tools. You hire the measurement and the fix, not another tutorial.

Can you fix our current RAG?

Usually yes. Most work is a surgical replace of chunking, hybrid retrieve, or verification. Full rebuilds are rarer.

What models do you use?

Whatever wins on measured faithfulness for the corpus. GPT, Claude, Command, or local via Ollama when residency requires it.

Do you ship SSO and permission filters?

As a scoped engagement, yes. The public demo is a single corpus. Do not read the marketing v1 claims as a live multi-tenant product.

What happens after the audit?

You keep the report and the code. Optional retainer for live eval and a monthly score your stakeholders can read.
